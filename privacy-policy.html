Privacy Policy

Wiz Reels Tracker & Counter by Wiz Labs · Effective 26 November 2026 (version 1.1.0)

Summary

Wiz Reels Tracker & Counter counts how many short videos you scroll past and can limit them. It has no accounts and no analytics. Your counts, settings and history stay on your phone, and the app never sends them to Wiz Labs or anyone else. The app uses the internet only for Google Play updates. No ads are shown in this version.

How the app works

The app uses Android's Accessibility Service to notice when you scroll to a new item in the short-video feeds of Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat and TikTok. It counts those items and the time you spend there, shows your totals, and can show a stop screen when you reach a daily limit you set.

Accessibility service

The service is limited by Android to those five apps. It sees which app is open, that a list scrolled, and how far. It does not read captions, usernames, comments, messages, passwords or anything you type, and it does not record or store what is on your screen. You can turn the service off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.

What is stored on your phone

  • Counts: for each app and each day, the number of reels, the seconds spent, and reels per hour. Kept for up to 400 days.
  • Settings: your limit, which apps are counted, haptics, and the time you accepted the Accessibility disclosure.
  • Share card and widget: if you tap Share, the app draws a picture of your day on your phone and passes it to the app you choose; nothing is sent until you choose. The home-screen widget reads your stored counts on your phone.

All of this stays on your phone. It may be included in your own Android backup if you have backup turned on. You can erase every count at any time with Settings, Delete all data.

Permissions

The app asks for notifications (Android 13 and later) to show quiet limit warnings. It has no location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera or phone permission. The app declares internet and network-state access so Google Play updates can work. It does not use the network for your counts. This version contains no ad software and shows no ads. A later version may show ads provided by Google AdMob; if that happens, this policy will be updated first.

Google Play components

The app uses Google Play In-App Updates and In-App Review, which are handled by the Google Play Store app under Google's Privacy Policy. This version contains no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting service.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
